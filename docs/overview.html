RockWizard

Pick a song and RockWizard writes a playable YARG chart for it: guitar, bass, drums and vocals, on every difficulty. It runs on a home PC, friends use it from their phones, and the charts land on the Steam Deck by themselves. This page lists what it does, how its models have improved, and what's next.

How a chart gets made

Find a songSearch by name, paste a YouTube link, or upload your own file.
Split itBS-RoFormer separates guitar, bass, drums and vocals into their own stems.
Write notesTrained models listen to each stem and place the notes; Whisper writes the lyrics.
Tidy itA beat grid, repeated sections charted alike, then Hard, Medium and Easy.
ReviewWatch the chart on the note highway in Staging, then publish.
PlayIt goes to Quickplay and syncs to the Steam Deck through Syncthing.

Features

Everything built since Oct 23, grouped by what you do with it.

Making charts
  • Search firstFinds existing community charts, song details and YouTube sources in one search.
  • Suggested songsA new batch every week from the iTunes charts, bands you already play and hand picks, with clips to hear first.
  • Queue with votesOne song at a time on the graphics card; friends vote for what goes next.
  • Every difficultyHard, Medium and Easy are cut down from Expert by a model trained on how human charters do it.
  • Vocals with lyricsA PART VOCALS track with pitched notes, lyrics and phrases, for YARG.
  • Repeats charted alikeA chorus plays the same every time it comes back, like a human chart.
  • Difficulty ratingsEach part gets a 0–6 rating the game shows.
Reviewing and the library
  • Note highwayWatch any chart scroll by with the song, for guitar, bass or drums.
  • Staging, then QuickplayNew charts wait in Staging until you mark them good and publish.
  • RechartRemake one song, Cleaner (fewer notes) or Busier (more fast notes).
  • Update chartsRemakes the songs that are behind today's models, backing up the old chart first; hand-edited charts are left alone.
  • Tidy, duplicates, coversFixes "Artist - Title" names, finds duplicate songs, and fetches missing album art.
  • Version recordsEvery chart notes which models and charting version made it.
  • Play ratingsRate a chart after playing it; ratings tune how future charts are made.
Backstage with friends
  • Join from a phoneScan a QR code on the same Wi-Fi (or over Tailscale away from home); it installs like an app.
  • Profiles and avatarsA builder or your own photo; songs you add carry your name.
  • BandsNames (or roll the dice), logos, band pictures, roles, set lists and a band tee.
  • GigsGig nights, battle of the bands, likes and posters in several styles.
  • Tonight's setlistOne shared list everyone can add to.
Scores
  • YARG scores import themselvesFrom YARG on the PC and, through Syncthing, on the Steam Deck. Only new bests are logged.
  • Linked profilesEach YARG profile is matched to a person here.
  • Difficulties that countPlay only Expert? Easy plays on your profile won't count.
  • Hand your profile overSomeone else playing as you? Pick them, and their plays go to them.
  • Top performancesAverages per instrument and difficulty, and your best songs.

Tried, and not kept

  • Song-wide pitch profileTelling the model the song's range made guitar and bass slightly worse.
  • Extra weight on fast runs and solosExact frets dropped from 0.233 to 0.221.
  • Stereo guitar input+0.005 exact frets: too small to be worth slower charting.
  • Higher-quality and BS-RoFormer drum inputRight-pad F1 fell (0.772 → 0.746 on BS-RoFormer stems).
  • Choosing the chord part or the riff per barRun 10: it chose correctly 84% of the time but charts came out the same (0.281 vs 0.283). Even perfect choices would only lift timing from 0.857 to 0.860.
Now training · run 11

Guitar and bass on the bigger set, and guitar learns taps

Same recipes as Guitar 7 and Bass 5, trained from scratch on 959 songs instead of 790, so the extra data is the only change. Guitar also gets a tap output, so charts can have tap notes where human charters use them (tapping riffs and solos). Guitar finishes around 7:50 PM, bass around 11 PM, then both are compared with the app's models. Nothing switches over without a look at the numbers.

More songs, better notesAbout a fifth more charts to learn from, many of them newer styles. Hoping exact frets climb past 0.281 without losing timing.
Tap notesA new note type for our charts. Partway through training it already finds taps at 0.43 F1 on validation songs, up from 0.
Bass from more variety15 more bass charts plus fresher stems. Hoping to edge past 0.476 exact frets.
